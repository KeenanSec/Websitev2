This demonstration proposes a simple notebook template. It does not describe a finished software project.

## Record the question first

A lab note should make the purpose of an experiment clear before listing the commands. Start with the question, then record the environment and your prediction.

```markdown
# Experiment title

## Question
What am I trying to understand?

## Environment
Versions, configuration, and isolation boundaries.

## Prediction
What do I expect to observe?

## Evidence
What actually happened?

## Next question
What remains uncertain?
```

## Keep facts and guesses separate

An output is an observation. An explanation of that output is an interpretation. Keeping the two separate makes it easier to revise a conclusion later.

## A possible next step

Try the template on a harmless local experiment. Ask someone else to follow the note. Their questions will reveal which assumptions need to be written down.
